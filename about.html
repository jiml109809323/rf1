About Stalls for Good

Stalls for Good is a simple idea from Robyn and Frances: make small stalls that bring people together and help charities do good things.

💜

Why it exists

A stall can be tiny, home-made, and still full of heart. The site helps families think of ideas, choose a cause, and share what they made.

🤝

What matters

Be kind, keep it safe, ask a grown-up, and donate through official charity pages. The point is helping, not making everything perfect.

Made with small hands and big ideas

This site is intentionally bright, friendly, and easy to use. It is for children and families who want a little nudge to do something generous together.
