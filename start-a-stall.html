Start a Stall

A practical little checklist for planning, decorating, selling, and donating safely.

How to set up your stall

💡

1Choose your idea

Pick a bake sale, craft stall, toy sale, games table, or your own brilliant idea.

💙

2Pick a cause

Choose a charity you care about and check where donations should be sent.

✨

3Make it welcoming

Use a colourful cloth, a clear sign, price labels, and a safe money jar or box.

📝

Planning checklist

  • Ask a grown-up before setting up or selling anything.
  • Choose a safe place, like a garden, school fair, or community event.
  • Make a simple sign that says what you are raising money for.
  • Keep food covered and label anything with common allergens.
🫙

Donation tips

  • Use a labelled jar, box, or QR code from the charity if they provide one.
  • Count money with a grown-up when the stall is finished.
  • Donate directly through the charity website or official instructions.
  • Take a photo for the gallery before everything gets packed away.
