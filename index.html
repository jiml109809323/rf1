How it works 💕

Three simple steps to create your stall and support a cause.

🎪

1Pick a Stall Idea

Choose something fun like a bake sale, craft stall, toy sale, or games stall.

💙

2Choose a Charity

Pick a charity you care about and learn how your support can help.

🫙

3Raise Money & Donate

Run your stall, collect donations safely, and send the money to your chosen charity.
